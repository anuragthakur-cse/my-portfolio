MeasureX — Digital Metrology Verification System

Team NEXMET | Problem Statement ID: SIH26036

An end-to-end digital verification platform designed to replace fragmented manual paperwork in weighing and measuring instrument stamping under the Legal Metrology framework.

Node.js Express.js MongoDB Bootstrap Cloudinary Geolocation API Dynamic QR Verification
  • Physical Instrument Traceability: Ties physical hardware ID and photo uploads directly to centralized records.
  • Geo-Tagged Inspection Trail: Enforces GPS coordinates and exact timestamps during physical on-site checks.
  • Dynamic Public QR: Citizens and owners can scan instant QR codes to verify equipment validity without logging in.
